# UpliftEd — Figma Make Website Prompt

---

## OVERVIEW

Design a complete, modern, child-friendly yet professionally credible website for **UpliftEd** — a life skills and character development initiative based in India that teaches children in Grades 3–5 (ages 8–11) at RMC Government Schools in Rajkot, Gujarat.

The website must simultaneously speak to four different audiences:
1. **Schools and principals** — build trust, explain the program structure
2. **NGOs, CSR teams, and educational foundations** — show impact, invite partnerships
3. **Volunteers** — inspire them to join, explain the roles
4. **Parents and the general public** — explain what UpliftEd is and why it matters

---

## BRAND IDENTITY

**Name:** UpliftEd
**Tagline:** *Skills for life. Character for always.*
*(Alternative tagline: "Where children grow beyond the classroom.")*

**Brand Personality:**
Warm, encouraging, optimistic, playful but thoughtful, child-centered, trustworthy, community-driven, inclusive, inspiring.

**Do NOT make it look like:**
- A corporate NGO (avoid cold blues and formal tones)
- A tutoring/academic coaching brand
- A government scheme

**It SHOULD feel like:**
- A modern, student-led social initiative
- Something a confident, well-organised 18-year-old founder built with real heart
- Professional enough for schools and NGOs, warm enough for parents and children

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Warm saffron/amber (#F5A623 or similar warm orange — echoes Indian identity and energy)
- Secondary: Deep teal (#1A7F6E — trust, calm, growth)
- Accent: Soft coral (#F07B54 — warmth, creativity)
- Neutral: Off-white (#FAFAF7) and warm dark (#2C2B28)
- Use white space generously. Avoid cluttered layouts.

**Typography:**
- Headings: Poppins (Bold / Semi-bold)
- Body: DM Sans or Inter
- Accent/Quote text: Fraunces or Lora (serif, for emotional pull quotes)

## DESIGN SYSTEM GUIDELINES

**Layout:**
- Max content width: 1200px
- Section vertical padding: 80–120px on desktop, 48px on mobile
- Use 12-column grid
- Generous white space throughout

**Components to design:**
- Navigation bar (logo left, links center/right, CTA button "Get Involved")
- Primary button (saffron background, dark text, rounded pill shape)
- Secondary button (outline, teal border)
- Skill card (icon top, title, description, soft tinted background)
- Impact stat (large number, small label below)
- Team member card (photo placeholder, name, role)
- Timeline node (dot + date + title + short description)
- Quote block (left border accent, serif text, attribution)
- Activity card (title, skill badge, short description)
- Section divider (subtle wave or geometric motif in warm tone)

**Illustration Style (if used):**
- Flat, minimal, Indian-flavored illustrations
- Children depicted in diverse clothing
- Warm color palette matching the brand
- No clipart. No generic stock icons.

**Mobile:**
- All sections stack vertically
- Navigation collapses to hamburger
- Cards go full width on mobile
- CTA buttons go full width on mobile

**Footer:**
- Logo
- 3-column links: Pages · Get Involved · Connect
- Tagline
- Copyright line

---

## TONE OF ALL COPY

- Warm, direct, and honest
- Never corporate or bureaucratic
- Never overly inspirational or preachy
- No jargon
- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Speak to the heart but back it up with structure.

Example of good tone:
> *"UpliftEd isn't a tutoring program. It doesn't reteach what schools already teach. It fills the gap — the one between academic knowledge and the skills children actually need to navigate life."*

---

## WHAT TO AVOID

- No stock photos of generic "happy children in classroom" unless specifically styled
- No dark or corporate color schemes
- No long blocks of unbroken text
- No animations that distract from content
- No cluttered hero sections
- No Comic Sans or overly childish fonts (this is a professional initiative, not a kindergarten flyer)
- Do not make it look like a donation-begging NGO website

---

## SUMMARY IN ONE SENTENCE

Design a warm, modern, professionally structured website for UpliftEd — an Indian student-led life skills education initiative — that speaks equally to children, parents, school principals, NGO partners, and potential volunteers, built around the 4Cs framework and grounded in three years of real community impact.
